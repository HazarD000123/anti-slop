# Human copy

- **True facts only.** Never guess dates, user counts or statuses. Check the project, git history or the live site, or ask. Real numbers are the selling point; invented ones ruin it.
- **Say what it does for the person using it.** Skip architecture and internal stats visitors can't interpret.
- **No hype.** No metaphors standing in for facts, no "revolutionize", "seamless" or "elevate".
- **No AI rhythm.** No "it's not just X, it's Y", no reflexive lists of three, no closing summary lines, no em dashes.
- **Match their voice.** Read how they write in their messages and existing pages, then use that casing, slang and sentence length. Don't invent habits, opinions or personal details; ask for them.
- **Their words, not a reference's.** When matching another site, its copy never carries over.

Read every line out loud. If you wouldn't say it to a friend, rewrite it.
