# Moth: creative brief

Written with `/anti-slop:brief`.

**Point of view:** a dark room at 3am with one lamp on, and something keeping watch around it. Moth is the bot that stays up.

**Palette from a source:** everything comes from a lit bulb in the dark.
- Room `#0a0705`, a warm black from a dim room, never pure black
- Wing `#efe2cc`, paper-thin cream lit from one side
- Lamp `#ffb454`, the one accent, and it's literally the only light source

**Fonts:** Fraunces for anything with a voice, soft and slightly strange at huge sizes. Inter for anything that should disappear.

**Signature detail:** the swarm. Hundreds of moths around one lamp, re-forming as you scroll to show what the bot does. Everything else stays quiet.

**Motion:** the swarm carries all the motion. Text lands once and holds still. Reduced motion gets a calm, slow swarm with no camera travel.

**Structure:** one night, top to bottom: the lamp, the door (moderation), the room (music), the clock (reminders), let it in.

**Real content:** name and three features. No stats, users or testimonials exist, so none appear. The invite link is still needed.

**Avoid:** purple, gradient text, feature cards, stats rows, "supercharged".
